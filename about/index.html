About me

I’m Affaan, known online as cogsec. I’m 23 and co-lead compute trading at Jump Trading alongside Alejandro Soumah Fernandez. I created ECC and co-founded Itô Markets, which was acquired by Jump.

I started building PCs at 11 and trading CS:GO skins. That led to custom computers, electronics commerce and crypto.

By 20, I’d earned two B.S. degrees from UC San Diego, in Mathematics–Computer Science and Business Economics, while running my own business. At 21, I left graduate study in Applied and Computational Mathematics at the University of Washington to build full-time.

Running a company while studying pushed me to build the help I couldn’t delegate: an AI consultant, secretary and manager. That venture didn’t work commercially. The ambition carried into everything after it: seeing how much one person could build and operate with the right tools.

Since then, I’ve built trading agents live, worked on elizaOS, helped build prediction-market infrastructure, won the Anthropic × Forum Ventures hackathon with Zenith, and created ECC.

The path hasn’t been linear. Markets, hardware and AI now meet in the work I do at Jump.

What I’m building now
